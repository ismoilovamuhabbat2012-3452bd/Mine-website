
# Mine Website
Guidance for teenagers to solve their financial problems. Using simple sctructure of engagement and competance. Learning finance is bit easier with my kitten Kiki!
Having a partner, is a huge encourage for young people especially when you need a friend.

The reason why this website suitable for teenagers:
1. Teeangers can learn fast and can usee the ability in a practise, having a cute menthor can be a huge motivator
2. While everyone talks about the reasons, and the action, Our kitten tells what to do and what can be achieved.
3. Teenagers can see the progress and can be really confused with financial terms and this website would be helpful much more for them than adults
4. Saving money and actually earning money is really complicated thing for most of the people and if someone with frsh brain tries to learn how the economics effect our life without any encourgement and engagement.


Additionally, Design of the website was done with love and patience. This type of design with light shadows in black backround isn't really common among the normal "money based" colors, but that represents finding a light in a darkness. The lesson page was dona with bright colors, to tell the teenagers that learning is the only light in the night.
