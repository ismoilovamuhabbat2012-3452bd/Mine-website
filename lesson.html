Lesson 1: Understand your money

Learn where your money comes from and where it goes. Tracking your spending is the first step. Let's start with identifying your main goal of speding money and becoming rich. Write down the reasons. And now mark really really necessary things and erase everything else. (being cool, and showing up to your friends isn't necessary.) Now just imagine that you achieved your goal, and you are rich. How do you feel? That's the important lesson. Keep imagining and try to print your goal and put it somewhere that you can see it every day. That encoureges you to keep going and not give up.
